# GlucoseInsights Privacy Policy (synthetic)


---

## 1. About this policy

This policy explains how GlucoseInsights handles information when you use the GlucoseInsights mobile application and its connected services. GlucoseInsights helps you monitor your blood glucose and heart rate by collecting readings from compatible sensors and showing trends and alerts in the app.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect the following information:

- **Account information:** your name, email address, date of birth, and the password you choose when you create an account.
- **Health measurements:** blood glucose readings from your connected glucose sensor, heart rate readings from your connected wearable, and the time each reading was taken.
- **Information you enter:** meals, medication reminders, notes, and target glucose ranges.
- **Device information:** device model, operating system version, app version, and a device identifier.
- **Usage information:** which screens you open, which features you use, and crash reports.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- display your readings, trends, and daily summaries;
- send alerts when a reading is outside the range you set;
- remind you to take readings or medication;
- fix errors and improve the stability of the app;
- understand which features are used so we can improve them.

## 4. How we share your information

We do not sell your information.

We use cloud service providers to host the app and process data on our behalf. Your readings and account information are processed on infrastructure provided by Amazon Web Services, and app authentication and messaging are handled through Google Firebase. These providers process your information only to provide their services to us.

If you choose to connect your account to a clinician or caregiver from the Sharing screen, we share your readings and summaries with that person. Sharing starts only after you send the invitation.

## 5. Your choices

- **Notifications:** you can turn alerts and reminders on or off in Settings → Notifications.
- **Analytics:** you can turn off usage analytics in Settings → Privacy. Crash reports are still sent so we can fix errors.
- **Sensors:** you can disconnect a glucose sensor or wearable at any time in Settings → Devices. No new readings are collected from a disconnected device.
- **Sharing:** you can stop sharing with a clinician or caregiver at any time from the Sharing screen.

## 6. How we protect your information

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted using TLS. Health measurements are encrypted when stored on our servers. Access to production systems is limited to authorized team members and requires two-factor authentication.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make changes, we will update the date below and notify you in the app.

## 8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact privacy@glucoseinsights.example.
